# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first public release

- "You first" window on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for categories you choose (writing, mental math, facts, summaries, decisions, debugging) and your own phrases.
- Review mode ("Review my version") and compare mode, with an optional self-rating card.
- Weekly report: your own tries, accuracy of estimates and guesses, a neutral mirror of what you use AI for, PNG share card.
- Everything runs locally. The extension makes no network requests and collects nothing.
