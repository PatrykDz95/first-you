# Privacy Policy — First You

Effective date: 28 October 2026

## In short

- The extension works entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- It reads what you type into the AI chat only on your device, to recognise the kind of task. Your prompts are never stored and never sent anywhere by the extension.
- Your own tries and simple statistics are stored only in your browser and deleted automatically: tries after 30 days, statistics after 90 days.
- The author does not collect or receive any data from the extension.

## 1. Who is responsible

Patryk Dziedzioch, Poland. Contact: contact@klip-it.app.

The extension does not send any data to the author or to anyone else. Everything described below happens on your device, and the author has no access to it.

## 2. What happens on your device

**Where the extension runs.** Only on chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com. When you send a prompt from the main input field, the extension reads its text in memory to decide whether to offer the "You first" window. The text is not saved.

**What is stored in your browser** (extension storage and IndexedDB, on your device only):

- your settings and your own trigger phrases;
- one statistics entry per prompt, without its text: date, site, task category, rough topic, word count, whether the window was shown, what you chose, and your optional self-rating; deleted after 90 days;
- the text of your own tries, so you can compare them with the answer; deleted after 30 days;
- a daily counter and a flag for the weekly summary.

**What is never collected:** the content of your prompts, AI answers, your browsing history, or anything that identifies you.

**Review mode.** If you click "Review my version", your try is added to the message you send to the AI chat. From then on it is handled by that chat provider under its own privacy policy, as is everything else you send there.

**Your control.** In Settings you can export all of this data as JSON or delete it with one click. Removing the extension also removes it.

## 3. Sharing

The extension makes no network requests of its own. Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or profiling. The share card in the weekly report is created on your device and goes only where you decide to post it.

## 4. Your rights

Because the author receives no data from the extension, there is nothing about you on the author's side to access, correct or delete; all of it is on your device and under your control (section 2). If you write to the contact address, your message is used only to reply and is deleted when no longer needed.

You can always contact the author with questions, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country.

Residents of California and other US states: the author does not sell or share personal information, and does not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

## 5. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 16, and the author does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## 6. Security

Local data stays in your browser's storage, protected by your device and operating system account. The source code is public, so anyone can check what the extension does.

## 7. Chrome Web Store

The use of information received by the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## 8. Changes

If the extension ever starts sending any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes. Other changes update the effective date above.
